Best EOD Drawdown Prop Firms for NQ Traders

For NQ and MNQ traders, EOD drawdown is often easier to plan around than real-time intraday trailing drawdown. The goal is simple: protect your liquidation cushion, understand when the floor moves, and avoid buying an account that fails from one normal Nasdaq pullback.

Short answer

Lucid Trading is the best first look for many NQ traders who want EOD drawdown, fast payout access, and cleaner account-fee positioning. Apex is still the major benchmark because traders already know the name and it offers more-account scale, but Lucid has the stronger practical rule fit for many one-account-at-a-time traders.

Use code dutrading on Lucid only after confirming the final checkout price and current account rules on the official checkout.

Why EOD drawdown matters

  • The drawdown floor usually updates from end-of-day balance, not every unrealized intraday spike.
  • NQ traders get more room to let trades breathe, but the threshold can still be enforced in real time once set.
  • The real risk is buying a cheap account without enough cushion for your normal stop size.

How to use the calculator before buying

  • Enter the account size, current balance, high-water mark, and drawdown amount.
