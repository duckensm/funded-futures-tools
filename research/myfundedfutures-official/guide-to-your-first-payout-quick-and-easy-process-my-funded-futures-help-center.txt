Guide to Your First Payout: Quick and Easy Process
March 9, 2026
Requesting your
first payout
from a sim-funded account with MyFunded Futures is straightforward and efficient. This guide outlines the step-by-step payout process, eligibility requirements, and what to expect after submitting your request. Whether you’re new to prop trading or aiming for fast withdrawals, follow these steps to access your simulated profits seamlessly.
​
​
Step-by-Step Payout Process for Sim-Funded Accounts:
Confirm Payout Eligibility: Pass your evaluation and meet the requirements for your sim-funded account. For detailed eligibility criteria, review the
Payout Policy Overview.
Complete KYC Verification:
Log in to the MFFU dashboard
Click on (Personal Settings > KYC) and
Submit your Know Your Customer (KYC) details.
See our KYC/AML Policies here
.
Set Up a Riseworks Account: Create an account on Riseworks to receive your payouts.
Submit Your Payout Request: Visit the MFFU payout dashboard to submit your request.
Meet Minimum Withdrawal Amounts:
Flex Plans: Minimum withdrawal of $250.
Rapid Plans: Minimum withdrawal of $500.
Pro Plans: Minimum withdrawal of $1,000.
What to Expect After Submitting Your Payout Request:
Review to Payout Process: All payouts are processed in 6-12 business hours excluding holidays.
First Payout Agreements: If this is your first payout with MyFunded Futures, you’ll receive an email to sign agreements via Riseworks.
Payout Delivery: After signing, your payout is transferred to your Riseworks account. You can withdraw funds via bank transfer or cryptocurrency.
Support Available: If you have questions or need help, contact the MyFunded Futures support team for prompt assistance.
