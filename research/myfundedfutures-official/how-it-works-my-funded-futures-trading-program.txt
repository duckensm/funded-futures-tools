FAQ page
Is this a real funded account or simulated?
You can trade a simulated account or real firm capital. The funded account stage operates exclusively in a simulated trading environment. All trades are executed on a demo basis and do not involve real capital. Customers who meet the required performance criteria may, at our sole discretion, receive an invitation to access live capital trading through one of our affiliated companies. Such access is by invitation only and is subject to additional performance criteria, eligibility requirements, and the terms and conditions of the relevant affiliate.
How long does it take to get funded?
There’s no minimum time requirement.
You can pass the evaluation as fast or as slow as you want, as long as you meet the rules.
Some traders qualify in just a few days. Others take longer to stay disciplined. The timeline is entirely up to you.
What rules do I have to follow during the evaluation?
The evaluation is designed to reward consistency and risk management, not gambling.
You’ll trade toward a clear profit target while staying within defined drawdown limits and position sizing rules.
There are no hidden tricks, no time pressure, and no surprise rule changes.
Everything is clearly outlined before you start.
What happens after I pass the evaluation?
Once you pass, you move into a funded account and begin trading under funded rules.
At that point, the focus shifts from proving yourself to executing consistently and getting paid.
You keep 80% of the profits you generate, and you can request withdrawals based on your plan’s payout structure.
How do payouts work?
Payouts are based on the profits you generate in your funded account while following the rules.
Depending on your plan, you may be eligible for fast or even daily payout requests.
Requests are processed reliably and transparently. What you earn is what you can withdraw².
Can I change my account size or plan later?
Yes.
Many traders start with one plan or account size and adjust as their confidence and strategy evolve.
You can upgrade, add accounts, or switch plans to better match how you trade at any point.
