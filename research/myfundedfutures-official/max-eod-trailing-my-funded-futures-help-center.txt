MAX EOD Trailing
Max EOD
March 2, 2026
At
MyFunded Futures
, we wanted to eliminate the daily drawdown so that our traders could relax without having to worry about the constant drawdowns affecting their accounts and mindset.
We do not have a daily drawdown but we will have a Max EOD (End of Day) Drawdown which will be set at 3%. Also to be noted the Max EOD trailing locks in at $100 plus the initial starting balance.
​
​
NOTE:
You
must be aware
that
open equity losses
are taken into consideration when calculating whether or not the account failed on this rule. This means that if an open position dips your account balance below the minimum account balance, your evaluation attempt will fail
