Evaluation Accounts
Traders may hold a maximum of ten (10) active Evaluation Accounts at any given time.
Sim-Funded Accounts – $25K and $50K Plans
Traders may maintain up to five (5) active Sim-Funded Accounts simultaneously when holding only $25K and/or $50K account sizes.
These account sizes may be combined in any combination of $25K and $50K, provided the total number of active Sim-Funded Accounts does not exceed five (5).
Note: Effective March 24th:
Traders are permitted a maximum of three simulated funded accounts of the 50k Flex Plan.
Traders with 50k flex plans before March 24th are grandfathered in and can have five 50k flex sim-funded plans.
Sim-Funded Accounts – $100K and $150K Plans
If a trader holds any $100K or $150K Sim-Funded Account, the maximum number of active Sim-Funded Accounts permitted is three (3).
This three-account limit applies across all Sim-Funded account sizes, regardless of plan type.
