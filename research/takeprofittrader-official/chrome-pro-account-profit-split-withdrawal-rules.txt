Profit Split
In the PRO account, the profit split is 80/20, where the trader keeps 80% of the profits.
Withdrawal Rules
When you reach your PRO account, you have the ability withdraw funds on day one. However, you must build a buffer on the account first.
You can withdraw your profits at 80% once you reach the level of your maximum drawdown which we refer to as the "buffer zone". For example, if you have a $50,000 account, your maximum drawdown or buffer zone is $2,000. In this case, you will need to reach a balance of $52,000 in order to start withdrawing at 80% of total profit.
Buffer Zones Per Account Size The buffer is equal to the maximum drawdown amount on your account.
